This was a redesign of how money moves through the consumer platform end to end. Purchase,
consumer pay-in, and publisher payout, all driven by a regulated entity standing up in the EEA.

The program arrived with real requirements ambiguity and no operating rhythm. I stood up the
governance it lacked, then designed and ran a five-day workshop to break a design that had
stalled. Authored the capability specs, workshop artifacts, daily summary communications, and
the gap analyses underneath the milestone, leaning on agentic tooling to move that volume of
documentation at pace. The consumer-side work also improved the commercial design, which
commercial team members acknowledged in review.
