by name. These title-plus-subscription platform capabilities proved competitively critical and
are now adopted across multiple publishers. Coordinating the global GTA VI release meant working
across eight-plus teams (pricing, ingestion, gaming insights, risk/fraud, groups platform,
marketing, and account management) through a rolling launch that followed midnight across every
time zone.
