Took end-to-end ownership of a space that had lost both engineering investment and business
alignment, and turned it into a platform three businesses actively invested in.

Validated retail expansion as platform strategy through a staged pilot ladder: a 2K device
pilot, then 10K, then additional markets. Enabled product bundling on the ROG Xbox Ally handheld
at launch by building controller navigation support into the claim flow and creating the
test-account path its validation team ran against.

The $10.8M recovery meant mobilizing a cross-functional team against a misconfiguration that had
expired live offers early, and protecting large OEM partner relationships in the process. The
$213M liability reduction came from shipping a dynamic device-based expiration capability.
